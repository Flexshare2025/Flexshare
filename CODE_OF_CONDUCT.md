# Code of Conduct

FlexShare welcomes respectful collaboration. Contributors and reviewers are expected to keep discussions professional, constructive, and focused on improving the project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give clear, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while still addressing problems directly.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on facts, trade-offs, and user impact.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private credentials, personal data, or sensitive project information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Posting secrets, credentials, or exploit details publicly.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Reporting

For sensitive conduct or security concerns, contact the maintainers privately instead of opening a public issue.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
